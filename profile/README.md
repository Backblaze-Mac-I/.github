## What Is Backblaze for Mac

backblaze mac is an online backup service designed from the ground up for the Mac. It solves the single
most common failure in personal computing — the lost, stolen or dead drive — by keeping a complete,
encrypted copy of your files in the cloud. Instead of asking you to pick folders, schedule jobs and
babysit an external disk, Backblaze simply backs up everything and keeps it current in the background.
With unlimited storage for a flat monthly fee and a free 15-day trial, peace of mind is within reach
without any real setup effort.

What follows is a feature-by-feature look at how backblaze for mac protects your data, from the always-on
backup engine to encryption, restores and locating a lost machine.

## The Automatic Backup Engine

The core of download backblaze for mac is an engine that protects your data by default. There are no
schedules to build and no folders to hand-pick.

- **Backs up everything** — documents, photos, music and settings are included automatically, not just a folder you remembered to select.
- **Continuous in the background** — new and changed files are uploaded as you work, so your cloud copy stays close to what is on your Mac right now.
- **No file-size limits** — large videos and disk images are backed up along with everything else.
- **External drives too** — attached USB and Thunderbolt drives can be backed up as part of the same plan.

Because online backup mac with Backblaze runs quietly and continuously, you never have to think about
whether today's work is protected. This always-on model is exactly what separates a real backup from an
external drive that is only as current as the last time you plugged it in.

## End-to-End Encryption

Security sits at the center of backblaze mac. Your data is encrypted on your computer before it ever
leaves, transmitted over a secure encrypted connection, and stored encrypted on the Backblaze servers.

- **Encrypted in transit** — files are protected on the way to the cloud over a secure connection.
- **Encrypted at rest** — your data stays encrypted while stored on Backblaze servers.
- **Personal encryption key** — an optional private key keeps control of decryption entirely in your hands.

That means your files are protected both in transit and at rest, so backing up sensitive documents never
means giving up privacy.

## Flexible, Accessible Restores

Backing up is only half the job — backblaze download mac is equally focused on getting your data back.
Restores are flexible and accessible from anywhere.

- **Browse online** — sign in from any browser to see and download your backed-up files.
- **Download what you need** — recover a single document or a whole folder without touching your Mac.
- **Mailed drive option** — for large restores, have your files overnighted to you on a USB drive.
- **Return-for-refund** — the mailed drive can be sent back for a refund after you have copied your data.

Because online backup mac gives you both instant web downloads and a physical mail-restore option,

## Locate a Lost or Stolen Mac

Beyond backup, backblaze for mac includes a Locate My Computer feature. Because the app checks in as it
backs up, the web console can show the approximate location of your Mac on a map. If your laptop is lost
or stolen, that same continuous connection that protects your files also helps you find the machine — and,
more importantly, recover the data on it from the cloud.
